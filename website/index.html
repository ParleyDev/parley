Voice & video

Drop in, talk, share your screen

Peer-to-peer calls that suit rooms of about six to eight. For bigger rooms, point Parley at a LiveKit server. Screen shares start when you click Watch Stream, so nobody downloads a stream they aren't looking at. Turn on end-to-end encryption for a channel or a DM and only the people in the call can see and hear it, not even the server, with a safety code you can compare out loud. Push-to-talk, noise suppression and per-device settings apply live.

  • Screen share
  • Watch Stream
  • End-to-end encryption
  • Camera
  • Push-to-talk
  • Soundboard

Your community, your rules

Roles, permissions and the tools to run the place

Coloured and gradient roles with a real hierarchy, per-channel overrides, private channels, timeouts, bans, member screening and an audit log. Custom emoji and stickers per server. Invite links that expire when you choose, from 30 minutes to never, with an optional use limit. List your server in Discover when you want new people to find it, and claim up to three vanity links like /invite/your-name. Put a live badge on your README or site, like the one at the top of this page: it shows who's online and opens your invite.

Engineers & band

Roles made for sessions

Engineers start and stop takes. Artists are heard and recorded. Everyone else listens from the audience until an engineer brings them in.

Hi-fi audio

Music doesn't get treated like speech

High-fidelity channels switch off noise suppression and echo cancellation, send stereo Opus at up to 510 kbps, and can drop to 10 ms frames for tighter timing.

Mix in the browser

Play back, solo, export

Every take opens in a multitrack player with per-track gain, mute and solo, lined up automatically. Export renders a 48 kHz, 16-bit WAV of the mix.

Channel 03 · Self-host

Your server. Five minutes.

Parley ships as two Docker images and a Postgres database. If your machine runs Docker, it runs Parley: a spare PC, a Raspberry Pi, a NAS or a VPS.

Step-by-step guides
Linux Raspberry Pi Windows macOS Proxmox Synology Unraid TrueNAS Portainer
~/parley
# grab the compose file and settings
$ curl -fsSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ParleyDev/parley/main/docs/self-hosting/compose.yaml
$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ParleyDev/parley/main/docs/self-hosting/example.env -o .env

# set two secrets, then your address and admin email in .env
$ sed -i "s/^POSTGRES_PASSWORD=.*/POSTGRES_PASSWORD=$(openssl rand -hex 32)/" .env
$ sed -i "s/^JWT_SECRET=.*/JWT_SECRET=$(openssl rand -hex 32)/" .env

$ docker compose up -d
 ✔ Container parley-db-1        Healthy
 ✔ Container parley-backend-1   Started
 ✔ Container parley-frontend-1  Started
  • HTTPS your way: built-in Let's Encrypt, Caddy, Traefik or Nginx Proxy Manager
  • No open ports: Cloudflare Tunnel or Tailscale
  • Backups: one command, or nightly, with a restore script
  • Upgrades: pull, restart, migrations run themselves
