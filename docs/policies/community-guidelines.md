# Community guidelines (template)

> A starting point. Replace everything in `[square brackets]`, delete what does not fit, and have it read by
> someone who knows the law where you operate before you publish it.

These are the rules for everyone on [name of your instance]. Servers here can add their own rules on top; they
cannot allow what these forbid.

## Not allowed, anywhere

**Harm to children.** No sexual content involving anyone under 18, real or drawn. No sexual talk with a minor, no
asking a minor for pictures, no arranging to meet one. We report this to the authorities and remove the account.

**Sexual content outside the places for it.** Adult content is only allowed in channels and servers marked 18+,
which are open only to people confirmed as adults. Never in profile pictures, banners, server icons, emoji or
stickers.

**Intimate pictures of someone without their consent**, including edited or generated ones, and threats to share
them.

**Threats and violence.** No threatening to hurt anyone, no encouraging others to, no praising attacks.

**Harassment and hate.** No targeting people for abuse, no dogpiling, no attacks on people for who they are.
Disagree with what someone says, not with their right to be here.

**Encouraging self-harm.** If someone is struggling, be kind and point them to help. Never encourage it.

**Other people's private information.** No posting someone's address, phone number, workplace or documents
without their consent.

**Spam and scams.** No mass messages, no fake giveaways, no phishing, no malware, no selling accounts.

**Getting round a ban**, here or in a server, with another account.

**Anything illegal** where we operate: [country or region].

## Ages

You must be at least [13 / the age that applies to you] to have an account here. Accounts of people younger than
that are suspended.

We do not ask for your date of birth. Until you are confirmed as an adult, age-restricted places are closed to
you and messages from strangers arrive as requests with pictures hidden. If you are under 18, please say so in
**User Settings → My Account → Age**: it turns on stronger protections.

## If you see something

Use **Report** on a message, a person or a server. Reports about children at risk, threats, self-harm and
intimate pictures are looked at first. If someone is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

You can also reach us at [safety contact].

## What we do about it

Depending on what happened: remove content, limit an account for a time (it can carry on where it is but not start
new conversations), suspend it for a time, or ban it. Server moderators can also time out, kick or ban within
their own server.

We tell you when we act on your account, unless the law forbids it. If you think we got it wrong, write to
[appeals contact] and a person who was not involved will look again.
